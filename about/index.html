About me

I'm Rich Tyson. I run CIE Insider on my own, so the trainers, the emails and the marking are all me.

  • BSc Physics (Hons), then a PGCE in Physics with Mathematics. I qualified as a teacher in 2015.
  • Former Cambridge examiner for IGCSE Physics (0625) and AS and A Level Physics (9702).
  • I've read every examiner report Cambridge has published for both, from 2021 to 2026. That reading is what the site is built on.

Teaching

Before I qualified, I was a physics and maths coach in a school, working with small groups and one to one. Most of my students were Year 10s at risk of exclusion, so we entered them for their exams early to make sure they left with qualifications.

I started my teaching career at The Blue Coat School in Liverpool, a grammar school with very high-achieving students. They asked hard questions and expected proper answers, so I had to know the physics well beyond the syllabus. That's where most of my subject knowledge was built.

Since then I've taught in other UK schools and at an international school, where my last classes sat Cambridge IGCSE and A Level. I've tutored extensively, and I've worked in edtech: I wrote and presented an IGCSE maths course on camera for an e-learning platform, then planned and structured its physics course.

My last IGCSE class

28 students took Cambridge IGCSE Physics (0625) with me. They also sat Biology and Chemistry, each with a different specialist teacher.

17 of 28

got an A* or A in physics.

26 of 28

got their best or joint-best science grade in physics. None did worse in physics than in both of their other sciences.

Because the same students sat all three sciences, each of them is compared with themselves. It's one class in one year, so read it as what happened, and no promise about yours.
