## 1. Where it goes: cieinsider.com. Buy physicswithrich.com today regardless.

**Put the offer on `cieinsider.com/marked/`.** Six reasons, in order of weight:

1. **You already decided this, on 2026-09-09.** The note in `TASKS.md` from the handle grab says it
   plainly: PhysicsWithRich is the personal and social front, CIE Insider stays the product and the
   domain, every bio points at cieinsider.com. This page is a product page. Re-deciding that two days
   later, for one asset, fragments a funnel that is already thin.
2. **The submission address is `rich@cieinsider.com`.** It is on `/intensive/` already. Landing them
   on one domain to email another is a seam a student notices.
3. **A brand-new domain has no trust to lend, and this offer needs trust more than any other asset
   you have.** You are asking a teenager to send you their worst work. "An examiner will mark this"
   is carried by the corpus, the examiner reports, the trainers and four years of position. A
   two-day-old domain carries none of it, and the page would have to spend its first screen
   rebuilding what cieinsider.com already has.
4. **Nothing is wired there.** No `gate.js`, no Kit form, no sitemap, no analytics beacon, no
   `/intensive/` to hand off to. You would be rebuilding plumbing to host one page.
5. **It feeds a CIE Insider rung.** The only paid thing this converts into is the Examiner Intensive
   at `/intensive/`. Same domain, one click, no context switch.
6. **At 477 subscribers and GBP 0 lifetime revenue, a second domain is overhead with no offsetting
   benefit.** Split SEO authority, two sets of pages to keep consistent, two places for copy to drift.

**Buy `physicswithrich.com` today anyway.** It is a red item on `TASKS.md` and has been since
2026-09-08, whois showed `.com` free on 2026-09-09, and it is the one part of the handle grab that
costs money rather than five minutes, which is exactly why it keeps slipping. Its job is to stop
anyone else owning the name your YouTube channel is about to spend months building. That job is done
by a 301, not by a site.

**Mechanically, in Cloudflare, and note what this does and does not touch:**

- Register through Cloudflare Registrar. This creates a **new, separate zone**. It touches nothing on
  the `cieinsider.com` zone.
- **The apex `cieinsider.com` stays DNS-only, grey cloud.** Proxying it breaks GitHub Pages'
  certificate. Nothing in this task goes near it, and nothing should.
- On the new zone: add a proxied placeholder record on root and `www` (an `AAAA` to `100::` is the
  usual discard target), then Rules, then Redirects, and point both at `https://cieinsider.com`
  with a 301, preserving path and query. Free plan covers this.
- Decide `.co.uk` in the registrar cart. The CLI whois on 2026-09-09 fell through to IANA rather
  than Nominet, so that check was inconclusive and the cart is the reliable place to find out.
- **You buy it. I do not purchase anything.**

## 4. The collision with `/intensive/` — resolve before either goes live

Both pages now describe the same free marked script, in different words, with different framing, and
only one of them has a cap on it. If both went live as they stand, a student could read that the free
script is an application to a GBP 199 tier on one page and a standalone free offer with twenty places
on the other.

Three ways out, my recommendation first:

- **A (recommended). `/marked/` owns the free script; `/intensive/` links to it.** Replace the
  application section on `/intensive/` with a sentence pointing at `/marked/`. One description of the
  offer, in one place, and the Intensive page gets shorter, which it can afford. It also puts the
  free thing in front of the paid thing, which is the whole argument of the audit doc.
- **B. `/intensive/` keeps its own application and `/marked/` drops the closing Intensive mention.**
  Two separate doors, no cross-reference. Simpler to ship, but you then run two free-marking offers
  with two caps and have to track which script came from which.
- **C. Ship only `/marked/` for now** and leave `/intensive/` in the drawer until the October gate,
  as `01-roadmap.md` originally had it. Cleanest if you are not ready to decide the GBP 199 question,
  and it loses nothing, because `/intensive/` is not live or linked either.
