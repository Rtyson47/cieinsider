What I mark on Paper 4

Paper 4 (A Level Structured Questions) is 2 hours and 100 marks. The questions are set on the A Level topics, 12 to 25, from motion in a circle to astronomy and cosmology, and the syllabus says knowledge of the AS content will be required too. Half the marks test what you know and half test how you use it.

What it testsWhat the syllabus says it testsWhat I check on your script
Knowledge and understanding
AO1, half the marks		Phenomena, facts, laws, definitions and theories, and the vocabulary, symbols, quantities and units that go with them.Every definition and recall answer word by word against the scheme, and every unit, including the ones you have to convert, such as joules to electronvolts.
Handling and applying information
AO2, half the marks		Handling information in words, symbols, graphs and numbers: manipulating data, drawing conclusions, and giving reasoned explanations.Every calculation line by line, each step of a show-that, every graph and sketch, and every explanation against the points the scheme lists.
