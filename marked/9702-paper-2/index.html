What I mark on Paper 2

Paper 2 (AS Level Structured Questions) is 1 hour 15 minutes and 60 marks. The questions are set on the AS topics, 1 to 11, from physical quantities and units to particle physics. Half the marks test what you know and half test how you use it. I go through the scheme for your paper and place every mark on your script.

What it testsWhat the syllabus says it testsWhat I check on your script
Knowledge and understanding
AO1, half the marks		Phenomena, facts, laws, definitions and theories, and the vocabulary, symbols, quantities and units that go with them.Every definition word by word against the scheme, every unit, and every recall answer, such as quark charges.
Handling and applying information
AO2, half the marks		Handling information in words, symbols, graphs and numbers: manipulating data, drawing conclusions, and giving reasoned explanations.Every calculation line by line, each step of a show-that, and every sketch or graph, checked against the physics you worked out elsewhere on the paper.

Questions about Paper 2 marking

Which Paper 2s can you mark?

Any 9702 Paper 2 from March 2021 to June 2026 except one. That's 42 papers, and I hold the question paper for all of them and the published mark scheme for 41. Cambridge has not published the mark scheme for June 2026 Paper 21, so that's the one I can't mark properly: sit Paper 22, 23 or 24 from the same series instead. If you've already paid for a 21, tell me and I'll refund you.

Do I have to send the whole paper?

Yes. Sit it timed, 1 hour 15 minutes, and send every question as you wrote it, with your working and your sketches. On the sample, the calculations scored and the marks went on drawings and recall. That pattern only shows up across a whole paper.

What are the green cards?

Some marks score, but only just. Each of those gets a green card with the wording that makes it safe, because a stricter examiner could take it away. The sample has five cards, and three of them are marks I gave on the benefit of the doubt.

Will you tell me my grade?

I'll tell you where your mark sits against the grade thresholds Cambridge published for that paper, as in the sample: 47 out of 60 on 9702/23 in November 2024, exactly on the A line, or 44, a B, if the three benefit-of-the-doubt marks went the other way. That's where the script sits on that paper. It isn't a prediction for your exam.

Do you mark Paper 1?

No. Paper 1 is 40 multiple-choice questions, and its mark scheme is a list of answer letters, so you can mark it yourself. Paper 2 is written answers, where the wording decides the mark.
