One booklet per syllabus topic for Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics 9702 (2025–2027 syllabus), 25 in all, each with its own mark scheme. Two are free. The rest come as an AS pack, an A Level pack or all 25, in PDF or as editable Word files for teachers.

What’s in each booklet

Eight sections: the syllabus statements word for word as a red/amber/green checklist, key definitions, formulae and equations, diagrams to draw, worked examples, multiple choice, structured questions, and examiner tips that name the exam series and paper they came from. Each booklet says whether its topic is AS or A Level. The mark scheme is a separate file with the answers and model diagrams, so students can work first and check after.

Revising 9702 one topic at a time

The booklets follow the 2025–2027 syllabus topic by topic, so a student can rate every statement red, amber or green, work through the booklet, and rate it again. AS topics are 1 to 11 and A Level topics are 12 to 25, the same numbering Cambridge uses in the syllabus.

If you would rather practise than print, the free trainers cover the papers: the MCQ trainer for Paper 1, the AS definitions trainer and A Level definitions trainer, and the Paper 5 planning and analysis guide with its uncertainties trainer. The grade calculator works out the marks you need on the papers you have left. Taking IGCSE instead? The 0625 booklets are on the resources page.
