About this check

The jump from IGCSE to AS Physics is rarely the physics — it's the fluency

Cambridge AS Level Physics (9702) assumes, from the first lesson, that you can already handle a set of core numerical skills without stopping to think: writing numbers in standard form, swapping SI prefixes (nano, micro, milli, kilo, mega) for their powers of ten, rearranging equations before substituting numbers, quoting answers to a sensible number of significant figures, and converting units (including the squared and cubed ones). These are the skills that decide whether AS calculations feel quick or fiddly.

This is a free 10-question diagnostic covering exactly those skills, using the kind of quantities you'll meet at AS — momentum, charge, density, resistance, kinetic energy. It doesn't test the AS physics itself (you haven't been taught it yet). It tests whether the maths underneath it is automatic, and it's pitched at the level AS will actually ask for rather than the level IGCSE left you at — so a couple of questions are meant to stretch you. You'll get a readiness verdict and a per-skill breakdown showing where to spend your summer.

When you're ready to shore up the physics, the free AS definitions & formulae flashcards and the AS multiple-choice trainer are the natural next steps.
