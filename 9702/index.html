Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics · 9702

A Level Physics 9702, paper by paper

What each of the five components actually asks of you, how much it is worth, and a free trainer for the skills that are pure technique.

Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics is examined through five components. Three of them make up AS Level and can be sat on their own; the other two complete the full A Level. Two of the five — Paper 3 and Paper 5 — test experimental skill rather than physics content, which is what makes them the most trainable marks on the qualification.

Where AS ends and A Level begins

Three routes through the qualification

Cambridge sets out three ways to take 9702, and which one you are on decides what you should be revising this term:

THE ROUTES
  • AS Level only — Papers 1, 2 and 3, all in the same exam series.
  • A Level staged over two years — the AS papers in year one, then Papers 4 and 5 in year two.
  • A Level in one series — all five components together.

Whichever route you are on, the AS content is never behind you: Paper 4 assumes it, and Paper 5 assumes the measurement and graph work Paper 3 introduced. If you are arriving from IGCSE, the numerical handling is the usual first stumble — start with the AS readiness check.

Frequently asked questions

9702, quickly answered
What papers do you sit for Cambridge AS & A Level Physics 9702?

There are five components. AS Level is Paper 1 (Multiple Choice), Paper 2 (AS Level Structured Questions) and Paper 3 (Advanced Practical Skills). The full A Level adds Paper 4 (A Level Structured Questions) and Paper 5 (Planning, Analysis and Evaluation).

How long is each 9702 paper and how many marks is it worth?

Paper 1 is 1 hour 15 minutes and 40 marks. Paper 2 is 1 hour 15 minutes and 60 marks. Paper 3 is 2 hours and 40 marks. Paper 4 is 2 hours and 100 marks. Paper 5 is 1 hour 15 minutes and 30 marks.

How much is AS Level worth towards the full A Level?

The three AS components carry half of the full A Level: Paper 1 is 15.5%, Paper 2 is 23% and Paper 3 is 11.5%. The two A Level components carry the other half: Paper 4 is 38.5% and Paper 5 is 11.5%.

Can you take AS Physics without completing the full A Level?

Yes. Cambridge sets out three routes: AS Level only, with all AS components taken in the same exam series; the A Level staged over two years, with the AS components in year one and Papers 4 and 5 in year two; or the full A Level with every component taken in the same series.

What is the difference between Paper 3 and Paper 5 in A Level Physics?

Paper 3 is the laboratory paper: practical work and structured questions, 2 hours and 40 marks. Paper 5 is a written paper of two compulsory questions on planning, analysis and evaluation, 1 hour 15 minutes and 30 marks. Both are based on the experimental skills in the practical assessment section of the syllabus, and the context of the questions may sit outside the syllabus content.
