Cambridge IGCSE & A Level Physics MCQ Practice

A free multiple-choice trainer for Cambridge IGCSE Physics (0625) and AS & A Level Physics (9702). Work through real past-paper questions with the question paper open beside the answer grid, mark instantly, and get a topic-by-topic breakdown of where you dropped marks — so your next revision session targets the right gaps instead of guessing.

Every question comes from a genuine Cambridge past paper. The trainer currently covers the March, May/June and October/November series from 2023 to 2025 — every variant — across three papers:

  • 0625 Paper 1 (Core) — 40 questions, 45 minutes
  • 0625 Paper 2 (Extended) — 40 questions, 45 minutes
  • 9702 Paper 1 (AS multiple choice) — 40 questions, 1 hour 15 minutes

How the trainer works

  • Practice mode — answer at your own pace, pause the timer, and reveal an examiner's note on any question you get wrong.
  • Exam-conditions mode — the timer runs without pausing and the paper auto-submits at zero, so you rehearse real pacing.
  • Instant marking — your score appears the moment you submit, with every wrong answer explained.
  • Topic breakdown — see which syllabus topics cost you marks, ranked, after every attempt.

Frequently asked questions

How long is Cambridge IGCSE Physics Paper 1?

IGCSE Physics 0625 Paper 1 is the Core multiple-choice paper — 40 questions in 45 minutes. Extended candidates instead sit Paper 2, also 40 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes.

How many questions are on AS Physics Paper 1 (9702)?

Cambridge AS Level Physics 9702 Paper 1 is a multiple-choice paper with 40 questions sat in 1 hour 15 minutes, and it counts towards both your AS and full A Level grade.

What's the difference between Core and Extended?

Core candidates sit Paper 1 and target grades C–G; Extended candidates sit Paper 2 and can reach grades A*–E. Extended covers the full syllabus, including supplement content Core students don't take.

Are these real Cambridge past papers?

Yes — every question is taken from genuine Cambridge IGCSE and AS Physics past papers from the 2023–2025 exam series, shown alongside the original question paper.

Is the MCQ trainer free?

Yes, it's completely free to use. CIE Insider builds free, examiner-grade revision tools for Cambridge Physics students.
