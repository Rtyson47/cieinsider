The 10 mistakes that cost Extended Paper 2 candidates marks.

Compressed from five years of Cambridge examiner reports (2021–2025). The slips that separate A* from A.

40 questions · 45 minutes · no negative marking · never leave a blank

The 10 traps

Ranked by examiner-report frequency · 0625/21·22·23
01
Momentum is a vector — sign the rebound. +30 to −20 m/s is a change of 50, not 10. Set a + direction, sign each velocity, then subtract. Every series
02
Snell's law: take the sines. n = sin i / sin r — not i/r, not from the surface. Also n = 1 / sin c. Every June
03
Background count: subtract, halve, add back. Subtract background first; halve per half-life; add background back if the question wants total rate. Most-flagged
04
Wire resistance ∝ 1/d². R ∝ L/A and A ∝ d². Double the diameter ⇒ R × 1/4, not 1/2. Go via area. Recurring
05
Boyle's law: pV = constant (inverse). Double the volume ⇒ pressure halves. Inversely, not directly, proportional. Recurring
06
Transformer core carries flux, not current. Needs a.c. (DC = no changing flux). a.c.→d.c. is a diode, not a transformer. IₚVₚ = IₛVₛ. Several series
07
Lenz: the induced effect opposes the change. Right-hand rule = generator; left-hand = motor. Max EMF when flux changes fastest. Recurring
08
Beta = an electron from the nucleus. n → p + e, not an outer-shell electron. β−: A same, Z+1. α: A−4, Z−2 (neutrons −2, not −4). Recent series
09
Mass on a spring: resultant = T − W. Use tension minus weight, not tension alone — gravity is still acting. Seen 2025
10
Unit prefixes in E=VIt, P=IV, Q=It. mA→A and min→s, often both in one question. The classic factor-of-1000-or-60 slip. Recurring

6 habits that fix most of them

  1. Cover the options. Solve first. Trial-and-error from A–D is the #1 examiner gripe at every level.
  2. Set a positive direction; sign every vector. Momentum, velocity, impulse, force balance — draw the + arrow first.
  3. Write the formula symbolically, then substitute. Prevents the Snell's-law flip and ½mv² vs ½(mv)².
  4. Convert every unit to SI before substituting. mA→A; min→s; cm³→m³. Square / cube after converting.
  5. Multi-step? Finish every step. Subtract → halve → add back. Examiners flag candidates who stop one step short.
  6. Never leave a blank. No negative marking — eliminate two, then pick.

Exam morning, in order
  1. Read each question in full before looking at the options.
  2. Underline NOT · EXCEPT · ONLY.
  3. Set a + direction; sign every vector.
  4. Convert units to SI, then finish every step.
  5. If stuck — eliminate, mark, move on. Come back.
  6. Never leave a blank.
