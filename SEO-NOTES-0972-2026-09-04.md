## Why there is no `/0972/` page tree

You asked whether to duplicate the 0625 pages under 0972 and divert them. **A redirecting URL
never ranks - only its target does**, and `rel=canonical` excludes the canonicalised URL from the
index by design. Either way the mirrored tree earns nothing while doubling maintenance. Publishing
it *without* a redirect or canonical is worse: two byte-identical trees, Google picks one, and you
have no control over which.

You cannot rank twice for the same content. You rank once, with the page that best answers the
query - so the way to reach 0972 searchers is a page genuinely *about* 0972. That is this post,
and it carries an answer nobody else has published.

Breadcrumbs on the two guide pages were left alone deliberately: they mirror the BreadcrumbList
JSON-LD, and editing the visible crumb without the schema desyncs them.
